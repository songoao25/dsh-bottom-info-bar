- Provider, model, real balance or subscription quota, and this session's spend, on one line under the composer
- Picks balance, subscription quota, or cloud bill per provider automatically, with no manual switching
- Tracks spend for this session, today, the last 30 days, and all time; figures stay on this machine
- Click a field to switch between the full and compact layouts
- Quota credentials for supported providers (Alibaba, Kimi, MiniMax, Xiaomi, Zhipu, Command Code…) live under "Quota credentials" in the plugin settings: collapsed by default, bound providers first, the rest behind "Other providers"

Setup and troubleshooting: [install guide](https://github.com/songoao25/dsh-bottom-info-bar/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md).
